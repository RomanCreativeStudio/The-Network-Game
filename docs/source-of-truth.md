## 1. GAME IDENTITY

The Network is a multiplayer Roblox business/life simulation RPG where players build careers, relationships, wealth, influence, and organizations inside a persistent player-driven corporate ecosystem.

Players can:

- Start with nothing
- → build a career
- → rise through leadership
- → create their own organization
- → compete with other organizations
- → expand
- → merge/acquire
- → become a major corporation
- → defend their position
- → potentially fall
- → rebuild.

The Network is inspired by the successful corporate roleplay/progression aspects of *The Company*, but is **NOT** intended to be a clone.

**Core differentiation:**

*The Company* is primarily about climbing an existing corporate ladder.

*The Network* is about eventually having the ability to build, control, challenge, or reshape the ladder itself.

---

## 2. CORE PLAYER FANTASY

The player should constantly feel:

> "I'm building something that matters."

Multiple valid fantasies exist.

- **EMPLOYEE** — "I want to build my career."
- **LEADER** — "I want to prove I'm capable of running things."
- **EXECUTIVE** — "I want my decisions to affect the organization."
- **ENTREPRENEUR** — "I want to stop working for someone else and build my own organization."
- **FOUNDER** — "I want to turn my startup into something massive."
- **INDUSTRY LEADER** — "I want to compete with the biggest organizations in the world."
- **RIVAL** — "I want to take them down."
- **LAWYER/JUDGE** — "I want to influence the world through the legal system."
- **CORPORATE LEADER** — "I want to control and protect an enormous organization."

No single path is the correct way to play.

---

## 3. CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP

The fundamental loop is:

Work → Make decisions → Perform → Earn resources → Build reputation/influence → Advance → Gain responsibility → Interact with players → Make larger decisions → Create or join organizations → Compete → Grow

Eventually: **Build your own Network.**

---

## 4. PROGRESSION PHILOSOPHY

**NON-NEGOTIABLE:**

**Performance > Time**

The game must NOT force players to grind an arbitrary number of hours simply to advance.

A skilled player should be able to progress quickly.

However:

**Rank = Responsibility**

Higher positions must not simply provide a larger number beside the player's name.

Higher positions introduce meaningful responsibilities.

**Initial conceptual progression:**

Associate → Specialist → Manager → Director → VP → Chief → Executive → CEO

Exact rank names, requirements, and progression formulas are **NOT YET LOCKED**.

**Conceptual responsibility:**

- **Associate:** Learn fundamentals.
- **Manager:** Manage people/projects.
- **Director:** Manage departments.
- **VP:** Make strategic decisions.
- **Chief:** Control major organizational functions.
- **Executive:** Make organization-wide decisions.
- **CEO:** Responsible for organizational performance.
- **Founder:** Build the organization itself.

## 5. CORE PLAYER RESOURCES

The following resources are foundational:

- **MONEY / WEALTH** — Personal financial power.
- **INFLUENCE** — Ability to affect organizational and social outcomes.
- **REPUTATION** — How trusted/respected the player is.
- **PERFORMANCE** — How effectively the player performs responsibilities.
- **CONNECTIONS** — Relationships and access to opportunities.
- **CAREER** — The player's professional progression.

Exact formulas and interactions are **NOT YET LOCKED**.

These resources must interact meaningfully rather than exist as meaningless numbers.

Players can:

- Join organizations
- Rise through organizational hierarchy
- Manage employees
- Create departments
- Open branches
- Set budgets
- Run projects
- Negotiate contracts
- Recruit players
- Fire/promote employees
- Compete with other organizations
- Found their own organization

**Long-term organizational actions include:**

- Mergers
- Acquisitions
- Buyouts
- Restructuring
- Liquidation

Exact mechanics are **NOT YET LOCKED**.

## 7. FOUNDER SYSTEM

One of The Network's defining mechanics is player independence.

A player who reaches sufficient wealth, reputation, influence, experience, and other eventual requirements can choose:

**FOUND YOUR OWN ORGANIZATION**

This is **NOT** simply another promotion.

The player leaves the existing hierarchy and becomes responsible for building something themselves.

**The conceptual loop is:**

Recruit → Generate revenue → Manage expenses → Build reputation → Expand → Compete

Exact requirements are **NOT YET LOCKED**.

---

## 8. CORPORATE CONFLICT

Conflict is intentional.

Organizations can eventually:

- Compete for contracts
- Recruit employees from rivals
- Negotiate
- Form partnerships
- Challenge leadership
- Compete for influence
- Attempt acquisitions
- Merge
- Restructure
- Fail

Players can eventually experience:

- Leadership disputes
- Protests
- Strikes
- Internal political movements
- Board conflicts
- Elections
- Leadership removal
- Revolutions or similar player-driven organizational conflicts

**PRINCIPLE:** Drama should emerge from systems, not primarily from scripted events.

---

## 9. LEGAL SYSTEM

The Network will eventually contain a meaningful legal system.

**Potential case types include:**

- Contract disputes
- Employment disputes
- Corporate misconduct
- Fraud
- Abuse of authority
- Conflicts of interest
- Ownership disputes
- Acquisition disputes

**Potential roles include:**

Lawyer → Senior Lawyer → Corporate Attorney → Judge

**Cases may involve:**

Evidence → Arguments → Rulings → Appeals → Consequences

The legal system should actually affect players and organizations rather than being decorative.

---

## 10. ACCOUNTABILITY

Players should have significant power.

Power must have consequences.

**Example:** A CEO repeatedly fires employees to promote friends.

The system can record relevant actions.

Players can report potential misconduct.

An investigation may occur.

If misconduct is proven, potential consequences include:

- Reputation loss
- Wealth penalties
- Influence loss
- Position removal
- Organizational consequences
- Other appropriate penalties

The exact enforcement system is **NOT YET LOCKED**.

**PRINCIPLE:** Risk/reward gameplay is preferred over simply eliminating every form of bad behavior.

---

## 11. ANTI-BOREDOM PHILOSOPHY

There should never be one permanent "end."

Different stages create different problems.

- **STARTUP:** "How do I survive?"
- **GROWING ORGANIZATION:** "How do I expand?"
- **MAJOR ORGANIZATION:** "How do I compete?"
- **MEGA-CORPORATION:** "How do I maintain control?"
- **CRISIS:** "How do I prevent collapse?"
- **DEFEAT:** "How do I rebuild?"

**PRINCIPLE:** Being powerful should change the problems you face, not eliminate the problems.

Players should always have meaningful goals appropriate to their current stage.

---

## 12. ACCESSIBILITY PHILOSOPHY

Research into *The Company* identified a major recurring problem:

**Deep systems + confusing onboarding.**

The Network should follow:

**COMPLEX UNDERNEATH. SIMPLE ON THE SURFACE.**

New players should not need to understand:

- Mergers
- Acquisitions
- Corporate law
- Board politics
- Advanced economics

when they first join.

Systems should be introduced progressively as players gain experience and responsibility.

**Conceptual learning progression:**

- **First job:** Learn basic work/proposals.
- **Promotion:** Learn influence/progression.
- **Manager:** Learn personnel/management.
- **Executive:** Learn organization strategy.
- **Founder:** Learn business management.
- **Mega-corporation:** Learn governance, M&A, law, and large-scale strategy.

Exact tutorial and onboarding implementation is **NOT YET LOCKED**.

## 14. LONG-TERM VISION

**Conceptual ecosystem:**

PLAYER → CAREER → LEADERSHIP → EXECUTIVE → FOUNDER → ORGANIZATION → GROWTH → COMPETITION → EXPANSION → MERGERS / ACQUISITIONS → MEGA CORPORATION → POLITICS / GOVERNANCE / LEGAL CONFLICT → CRISIS → RESTRUCTURE / LIQUIDATION / COLLAPSE → REBUILD

## 15. THREE CORE PILLARS

Everything built should strengthen at least one of these pillars.

**PERSONAL** — "How do I grow?"
Career, Wealth, Influence, Reputation, Connections

**ORGANIZATIONAL** — "How do we grow?"
Organizations, Employees, Branches, Contracts, Expansion, M&A

**SOCIAL** — "How do we interact and compete?"
Politics, Alliances, Conflicts, Courts, Elections, Negotiations, Rivalries
